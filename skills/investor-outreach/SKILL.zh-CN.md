# Investor Outreach
# 投资人触达

Write investor communication that is short, personalized, and easy to act on.
撰写简短、个性化且易于执行的投资人沟通。

## When to Activate
## 何时激活

- writing a cold email to an investor
  - 撰写给投资人的冷邮件
- drafting a warm intro request
  - 起草温暖介绍请求
- sending follow-ups after a meeting or no response
  - 在会议后或无回应后发送跟进
- writing investor updates during a process
  - 在流程中撰写投资人更新
- tailoring outreach based on fund thesis or partner fit
  - 根据基金投资论或合伙人匹配定制触达

## Core Rules
## 核心规则

1. Personalize every outbound message.
   - 每一封外发信息都要个性化。
2. Keep the ask low-friction.
   - 保持请求低摩擦。
3. Use proof, not adjectives.
   - 用证据，不用形容词。
4. Stay concise.
   - 保持简洁。
5. Never send generic copy that could go to any investor.
   - 绝不发送可以发给任何投资人的通用文案。

## Cold Email Structure
## 冷邮件结构

1. subject line: short and specific
   - 主题行：简短且具体
2. opener: why this investor specifically
   - 开场：为什么是这个投资人
3. pitch: what the company does, why now, what proof matters
   - 推介：公司做什么、为什么是现在、什么证据重要
4. ask: one concrete next step
   - 请求：一个具体的下一步
5. sign-off: name, role, one credibility anchor if needed
   - 结尾：姓名、角色、如果需要一个可信度锚点

## Personalization Sources
## 个性化来源

Reference one or more of:
参考以下一项或多项：
- relevant portfolio companies
  - 相关的投资组合公司
- a public thesis, talk, post, or article
  - 公开的投资论、演讲、帖子或文章
- a mutual connection
  - 共同联系人
- a clear market or product fit with the investor's focus
  - 与投资人关注点明确的市场或产品契合

If that context is missing, ask for it or state that the draft is a template awaiting personalization.
如果缺少该上下文，请索取或说明草稿是待个性化的模板。

## Follow-Up Cadence
## 跟进节奏

Default:
默认：
- day 0: initial outbound
  - 第 0 天：初次外发
- day 4-5: short follow-up with one new data point
  - 第 4-5 天：用一个新的数据点简短跟进
- day 10-12: final follow-up with a clean close
  - 第 10-12 天：最后一次跟进，明确收尾

Do not keep nudging after that unless the user wants a longer sequence.
除非用户想要更长的序列，否则之后不要再催促。

## Warm Intro Requests
## 温暖介绍请求

Make life easy for the connector:
让连接者轻松：
- explain why the intro is a fit
  - 解释为什么这个介绍是合适的
- include a forwardable blurb
  - 包含一个可转发的简介
- keep the forwardable blurb under 100 words
  - 保持可转发简介在 100 词以内

## Post-Meeting Updates
## 会后更新

Include:
包含：
- the specific thing discussed
  - 讨论的具体内容
- the answer or update promised
  - 承诺的答案或更新
- one new proof point if available
  - 如果有的话一个新的证据
- the next step
  - 下一步

## Quality Gate
## 质量关卡

Before delivering:
交付前：
- message is personalized
  - 信息是经过个性化的
- the ask is explicit
  - 请求是明确的
- there is no fluff or begging language
  - 没有废话或乞求语言
- the proof point is concrete
  - 证据是具体的
- word count stays tight
  - 字数保持精简
